# Rank Advancement Progress: Yoli Brand Partner Dashboard

**Role:** Freelance product designer (solo)
**Status:** Pre-launch
**Tools:** Figma

## The problem

Yoli Brand Partners need to know where they stand, what's left to reach the next rank, and how they're tracking this bonus period. The existing experience made them work for that answer. [Add 1–2 sentences on the specific pain points from the original dashboard, plus the before screenshot.]

## Constraints

- 18 ranks, from Future Star to Triple Black Diamond
- Requirements are pass/fail, not continuous progress
- Bonus periods run on a rotating weekly color cycle that returning partners recognize
- Search is limited to a partner's own downline
- The platform hasn't launched, so no usage data yet

## Key decisions

**Steps, not percentages.** Requirements are pass/fail and weighted unevenly, so "60%" could overstate how close someone really is. "3/5 steps complete" is honest about what's actually done.

**No color-shifting progress ring.** Red-to-green framing signals failure before a partner has had a chance to act. The ring fills proportionally in a single brand color instead.

**Current rank as the hero.** The rank name is the identity anchor, so it gets the most visual weight in the widget.

**Outstanding requirements first.** Unfinished items move to the top with more emphasis. Completed items drop below, grayed and checked off, framed as progress locked in rather than tasks failed.

**A rank ladder dropdown with full context.** Achieved ranks stay visible but struck through, current rank is bold, and the selected target gets its own distinct state. The list opens centered on the partner's position, so a Triple Diamond never scrolls past 12 ranks to find themselves.

**One pattern for state labels.** "Sep 20 to Sep 27, 2026 (Current)," "Platinum (Next rank)," "Gold (Current)." Same structure everywhere, so partners learn it once.

**A fact, not a modal.** Highest rank achieved went from a button that opened a modal to one quiet line of text. A single data point didn't earn an extra click.

## What I cut, and why

This section is worth keeping because it shows restraint:

- The dynamic encouragement line — competed with the rank name for attention
- Confetti and completion badges — noise, plus ongoing maintenance for the badge
- The "Yellow Week" label — internal jargon, replaced by a small color dot
- Group headers and icons in the dropdown — strikethrough and bold already told the story
- A separate "Find a partner" button — merged into the Brand Partner field
